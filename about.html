Who We Are

A focused catalog for pond and farm equipment

Jay Kay Aqua Tech is organised around a simple idea: aquaculture teams shouldn't have to piece together aeration equipment, floating infrastructure and pond-care products from scattered suppliers. This catalog brings the categories a working pond needs into one place.

This website represents Phase 1 of the Jay Kay Aqua Tech online presence — the product structure and browsing experience. Company history, certifications and detailed credentials will be added once confirmed by Jay Kay Aqua Tech.

About Jay Kay Aqua Tech

Aquaculture equipment, sourced and presented with the farm in mind

Jay Kay Aqua Tech focuses on aeration and pond-support equipment for aquaculture operations — from paddle wheel aerators to floating platforms, probiotics and chemical ranges used across day-to-day pond management.

This site is Phase 1 of the Jay Kay Aqua Tech online catalog: a clear, browsable structure for the product range, with company details to be finalised as they're confirmed.
